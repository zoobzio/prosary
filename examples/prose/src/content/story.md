Solstice Rail started in 2019 with one train, one route and a question: why
does a journey of eight hundred kilometres have to begin at an airport at five
in the morning?

The first train ran from Paris to Vienna on a Thursday in October. Forty-two
people were on board, and one cat in a basket in the couchette coach. Most of
them had never slept on a train; all of them arrived rested, in the centre of
the city, with the whole day ahead of them.

### What we believe {#belief}

Travel should begin when you sit down, not when you land. A night train turns
the dead time of a journey into an evening and a night's sleep, and leaves the
daylight where it belongs: at your destination.

:::callout{kind="note"}
Every train in our fleet runs on electricity, and every route we add has to
replace a flight that people actually take. If it does not, we do not run it.
:::

### Where we are going {#ahead}

Today there are four routes, eight cities and seven countries. Next year there
will be more. We publish every change in the [journal](#journal), and the
timetable on this page is always the one we run.
