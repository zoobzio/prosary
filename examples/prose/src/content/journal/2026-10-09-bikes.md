---
title: Twelve bicycles on every train
date: 2026-10-09
author: Ines Marlow
---

Until now a full-size bicycle could travel on the Paris–Vienna line only. From
this week every train has a bike compartment with room for twelve, and a
reservation costs nothing with your ticket.

We counted: in September, one traveller in nine brought a bicycle on the
routes that allowed it. We expect that to grow, and we have left room in the
compartment to grow with it.
