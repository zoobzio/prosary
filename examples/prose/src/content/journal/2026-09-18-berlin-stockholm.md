---
title: A fourth route, Berlin to Stockholm
date: 2026-09-18
author: Ines Marlow
---

From December the night train leaves Berlin four evenings a week and arrives in
Stockholm in time for a late breakfast, by way of the [Øresund bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%98resund_Bridge). It is our
longest route yet, just under sixteen hours, and the first with a cabin coach
on every departure.

Bookings open today for travel from 4 December. The first week is already a
third full.
