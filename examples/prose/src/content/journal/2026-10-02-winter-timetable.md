title: The winter timetable
date: 2026-10-02
author: Tomas Reyes
---

The winter timetable runs from 13 December to 27 March. Three things change.

Munich–Rome goes from five nights a week to six, with the extra departure on
Saturday. Amsterdam–Zurich leaves twenty minutes later, at nine, so that
connections from the north make it. And on every route the bar coach now
serves until one in the morning.

The times on this page are already the winter ones.
