The short version. Each point here stands for a longer one in the full
conditions, which we send with every ticket.

### Tickets {#tickets}

A ticket is valid for the train, date and fare it names. It may be changed
free of charge until the evening before departure and refunded, less a fixed
fee, until departure itself.

### Luggage {#liability}

Two bags and a folded bicycle travel free with every fare. We are liable for
luggage placed in the rack of your coach or in the bike compartment, and not
for anything left in the bar coach.

### Your data {#privacy}

We keep your name, your contact details and your journeys for as long as you
have an account with us, and nothing else. We do not sell them, and we do not
share them except with the operator of a line that runs part of your journey.
