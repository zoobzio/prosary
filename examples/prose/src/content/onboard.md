title: Life on board
description: What the night looks like, fare by fare.
---

Every train leaves after dinner and arrives before breakfast. What happens in
between depends on the fare you choose.

### Sleeping {#sleeping}

The seat coaches are dimmed at ten and stay quiet until seven. Couchettes and
cabins have made-up beds waiting for you, and an attendant on every coach
through the night. :badge[New] Cabins on the Paris–Vienna line now have a
shower at the end of the coach.

### Dining {#dining}

The bar coach serves hot food until midnight and coffee from six. Couchette and
cabin fares include breakfast; cabin travellers have it brought to their door
at the time they choose.

### Luggage {#luggage}

Bring what you can carry. Every fare includes two bags and a bicycle, folded or
in a bag, in the luggage rack of the first coach. Full-size bicycles travel in
the bike compartment and need a reservation.

:::callout{kind="tip"}
Before you board, keep these within reach. The rest can go in the rack.

- [x] Ticket, on your phone or on paper
- [x] Passport or ID card
- [ ] Earplugs, if you are in a seat coach

:::
