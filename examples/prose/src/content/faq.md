---
title: Questions
description: The questions we are asked most, answered.
---

The questions we are asked most. For anything else, the attendant on your coach
is awake all night.

:::question

### Can I bring a bicycle?

Yes. A folded bicycle or one in a bag travels free in the luggage rack. A
full-size bicycle needs a place in the bike compartment, which you reserve with
your ticket; there are twelve on every train.
:::

:::question

### What if the train is late?

Night trains cross several networks and we are sometimes held at a border. If
you arrive more than an hour late, we refund a quarter of your fare; more than
two hours, half. You do not need to ask: it comes back to the card you paid
with.
:::

:::question

### Can I travel with children?

Of course. Children under six travel free in a seat, couchette or cabin shared
with an adult. A cabin for a family of four is two adjoining cabins with the
connecting door open.
:::

:::question

### Is there a shower?

Cabins on the Paris–Vienna line have a shower at the end of the coach, and
every station we serve has one in the arrivals lounge for travellers on any
fare.
:::

:::question

### Can I change my booking?

Up to the evening before you travel, as often as you like, for free. On the
day itself a change costs the difference in fare, if there is one.
:::
